StudyHub

Your one-stop-shop for buying and selling second-hand textbooks

Buy and Sell Second-Hand Textbooks with Ease

Search for textbooks by title, author, and subject, and filter by condition, price, and location.

Features

  • User Profile and Dashboard: Allow users to create a profile, view their purchase history, and track their textbook inventory
  • Textbook Search and Filtering: Enable users to search for textbooks by title, author, and subject, with filtering options for condition, price, and location
  • AI-Powered Price Suggestions: Provide users with price suggestions based on market trends, book condition, and demand
  • Instant Campus-to-Campus Delivery Coordination: Allow users to coordinate textbook deliveries between campuses, with options for tracking and scheduling
  • Secure Payment Processing: Integrate secure payment processing to facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers
  • Ratings and Reviews System: Enable users to leave ratings and reviews for textbooks and sellers, promoting trust and accountability within the community
  • Admin Dashboard and Analytics: Provide administrators with a dashboard to track platform activity, monitor user behavior, and identify areas for improvement
